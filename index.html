About Me

Focus on simple innovation.

I'm a full-stack software engineer based in London with a passion for quality work. I work from end-to-end — product decisions, database design, deployment, and ongoing operations.

My flagship work is SSCourier, an international shipping business I founded and built myself — the website, the tracking portal, the internal warehousing tools — was designed and implemented by me, serving over 1,000 customers per month.

Experience

Where I've built.

Lead engineer and technical decision-maker behind a live, revenue-generating international logistics business.

Founder & Technical Lead
Shipping Solution Courier (SSCourier)
Remote · London, UK  ·  sscourierja.com
2023 – Present

International shipping from the USA and China to Jamaica. Built and maintained the entire technology stack.

  • Built the customer-facing website and online tracking portal, serving 1,000+ customers per month who book shipments and track packages in real time.
  • Designed and built an internal warehousing system that scans, logs, weighs, and tracks thousands of packages per month — replacing manual spreadsheet entry with barcode scanning and automated data pipelines, eliminating entry errors entirely.
  • Wrote Python automation scripts that generate invoices and send customer notifications automatically, saving up to 6 hours of admin per week.
  • Unified supplier, freight, and customs data into a single Postgres database, eliminating the need to copy records across 4 separate portals and dramatically reducing reconciliation time.
  • Own the full stack end-to-end: product decisions, database design, CI/CD, deployment, domains and DNS, and day-to-day customer support.
